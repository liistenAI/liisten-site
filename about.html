About

The conversation should do the compliance work.

Liisten is an Australian-owned compliance notetaker, built for the firms now brought inside the AML/CTF regime. We started from a simple frustration: the evidence a good practitioner needs is already in the client meeting — so why re-key it into a form afterwards?

Australia's "Tranche 2" reforms bring tens of thousands of accountants, lawyers, conveyancers, advisers and real-estate professionals into scope for customer due diligence. For a large bank, that's a compliance department. For a three-person firm, it's another night of paperwork after the client has gone home.

We didn't think the answer was a longer checklist. A careful onboarding conversation already surfaces almost everything due diligence asks for — who the client is, how they're structured, where their money comes from, why they're really here. The problem was never the conversation. It was that the conversation vanished, and the compliance record had to be reconstructed from memory.

So we built Liisten to keep the conversation — and turn it into the record.
Consent captured first. Transcribed and assessed onshore. Reviewed and signed by you.

Liisten records a consented, in-person meeting and produces a preliminary risk assessment mapped to the AUSTRAC factors — every rating backed by a verbatim quote and a timestamp. It's a first pass, not a verdict: you read it, adjust it with your professional judgement, and sign. The record is yours; Liisten just does the groundwork so you don't start from a blank page.

What we believe

The principles we build on.

Compliance software touches people's trust and their data. These aren't features — they're the lines we won't cross.

Consent comes first

Nothing is recorded until the client agrees, on-screen. A record built without consent isn't a record we'd want to stand behind — so we don't build one.

Onshore, always

Recording, transcription and assessment all happen in Australia. A client's meeting never leaves the country — data sovereignty isn't a setting, it's the default.

The practitioner decides

Liisten drafts; you decide. Every rating shows its evidence so you can check it, override it, and own the outcome. The AI never has the final word — you do.

Evidence over guesswork

A quote and a timestamp sit behind every judgement. If it can't be traced back to something the client actually said, it doesn't belong in the assessment.

Australian owned

Built here, kept here.

Liisten is Australian owned and runs on Australian infrastructure, in Sydney. That's a deliberate choice: your clients' most sensitive conversations shouldn't be processed on the other side of the world to meet an Australian obligation. When a regulator, a client, or your own conscience asks where the data lives, the answer is simple — here.
