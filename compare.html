Why the difference matters

A great notetaker still isn't a compliance tool.

General-purpose notetakers are excellent at what they're for. Client due diligence just isn't what they're for.

A summary isn't an assessment

A recap and action items don't weigh a client's money-laundering risk, cite evidence against the AUSTRAC factors, or give you a rating you can defend. Liisten does — and shows its working.

Built for sales calls, not client files

Generic tools are tuned for meetings, standups and CRM sync. Nothing in them maps to CDD, beneficial ownership, PEP signals, source of funds, or your record-keeping obligations.

Your client's data leaves the country

Most notetakers run on overseas infrastructure. Liisten transcribes, diarises and assesses entirely onshore in Sydney — a client's meeting never leaves Australia.

And the alternative

Faster than the form, too.

The other option is the one you know: a written file note and a risk matrix after every onboarding.

HoursMinutes

Instead of writing the file note and risk rating from a blank page, Liisten drafts both from the conversation itself. You review and sign — not transcribe, not fill in a matrix from memory. The admin that used to eat an afternoon happens while the meeting is still fresh.
