The basics

What Liisten is, and who it's for.

What is Liisten?
Liisten is an AI notetaker built for Australian Tranche 2 firms. It records a consented, in-person client meeting, transcribes and diarises it onshore, and turns it into an AUSTRAC-mapped preliminary risk assessment and a detailed file note — ready for you to review, adjust with your professional judgement, and sign.
Who is it for?
The professions now captured by Australia's AML/CTF reforms — accountants and bookkeepers, lawyers and conveyancers, financial advisers, and real-estate professionals. If you owe customer due diligence on new clients, Liisten is built for you.
Does it work for in-person meetings?
Yes — that's the point. Most AI notetakers are bots that join a video call. Liisten is built to record the real conversation happening across the desk, from your phone, with consent captured first.
Do I have to use it for every meeting?
No. It's designed for client onboarding and other CDD-relevant meetings where you need a risk assessment and a file note. Use it when the conversation is one you need to document.

Compliance & AUSTRAC

Where Liisten fits your obligations — and where it doesn't.

Does Liisten make my firm compliant?
It does the groundwork — documented, timestamped evidence and a preliminary risk rating mapped to the AUSTRAC factors. But compliance stays your responsibility: you review and sign every assessment, and Liisten doesn't replace your firm's AML/CTF program. See how it maps to your obligations →
Is the risk assessment final?
No — it's preliminary by design. Liisten drafts; your professional judgement decides. Your review and signature are what make it your firm's assessment.
Does it verify ID or run PEP and sanctions screening?
No. Liisten flags PEP signals that come up in the conversation, but it isn't a screening service. Formal identity verification and PEP/sanctions screening run through your own provider — AML/CTF screening services such as Annature. Liisten's job is to tell you when to escalate to those checks.
How long are the records kept?
Signed assessments and their supporting evidence are retained for seven years, in line with AML/CTF record-keeping obligations.

Consent, privacy & your data

Consent-first, and onshore from end to end.

Do clients have to consent to being recorded?
Yes. Consent is captured on-screen before any recording starts — Liisten is consent-first by design, so the record of consent sits with the meeting it belongs to.
How do I ask a client without making it awkward?
In one sentence, before you start: "New AML laws mean I need to keep a proper record of this conversation — I'll record it so I can give you my full attention. That okay?" The law makes the ask for you. Read the full guide, with scripts →
Where is my data stored?
Entirely onshore in Australia. Recording, transcription and assessment all happen in Sydney — your client's data never leaves the country.
Who can see the recordings and reports?
Only your firm. Meetings, transcripts and assessments are scoped to your firm's account and the practitioners in it.
Is it secure?
Yes — data is encrypted and processed onshore, with access limited to your firm. The AI models that assist with the assessment run within Australian infrastructure, not offshore.

Accuracy & what you get

You're always in the loop — and in control.

How accurate is the transcription?
Liisten transcribes and diarises the meeting for you to review — it's a starting point, not the final word. You can read the transcript and correct anything before you sign.
How are risk ratings determined?
By a published methodology, not a black box. Every assessment scores the same seven AUSTRAC-aligned factors, and every rating must cite a verbatim, timestamped quote from the meeting — if the conversation didn't cover a factor, it's marked "Not covered" with a suggested follow-up question, never guessed. The overall rating follows a worst-factor rule, and each assessment records the methodology version that produced it. Read the full methodology →
What if a rating looks wrong?
Every factor shows the verbatim quote and timestamp behind it, so you can check the reasoning in seconds. If you disagree, you override it — your judgement always wins, and the change is recorded against the assessment.
What do I actually get at the end?
The whole record of the meeting: the audio recording, a full timestamped transcript, and a detailed file note. New-client meetings also get a preliminary risk assessment mapped to the AUSTRAC factors. Review it, sign, and approve — Liisten never sends or files anything on your behalf without that approval.
What AI does Liisten use?
Transcription runs on Amazon Transcribe and the analysis runs on Claude, both in AWS's Sydney region — nothing leaves Australia. The AI is constrained by the methodology: it can only rate what was actually said, quote for quote, and it's instructed to be conservative. It drafts; it never decides. Your meetings are not used to train any AI model.

Access & pricing

Where we're up to, and how to get in.

How do I get the app?
Liisten is on the App Store now, with the Android app following shortly. Create your firm first — the app signs in with the same account — then install it from liisten.com.au/get on your phone.
What does it cost?
Meetings are credit-based — a general meeting (file note) is one credit, an onboarding meeting (file note plus risk assessment) is two, and every firm's first ten meetings are free. See the pricing page for pack prices in AUD.
Is there a free trial?
Every firm's first ten meetings are free — recorded, reviewed and fully visible — so you can try Liisten on real meetings before committing to anything.
