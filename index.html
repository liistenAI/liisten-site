What Liisten does for your firm

Compliance work, done in the meeting.

As AML/CTF obligations extend to accounting, legal, financial-advice and real-estate firms, the client conversation becomes evidence. Liisten captures it and does the groundwork — you review and sign.

01

CDD through natural dialogue

Establish Customer Due Diligence in the ordinary flow of the meeting — who the client is, how they're structured, where their funds come from — without reading a checklist aloud.

02

Know when risk is low

Liisten helps you determine when a customer's money-laundering / terrorism-financing risk is low — so a simplified due-diligence decision is defensible, with the evidence behind it.

03

An AUSTRAC-mapped preliminary risk assessment

Every onboarding meeting produces a preliminary risk assessment mapped to the AUSTRAC framework — transcribed, diarised, and evidence-cited with a verbatim quote and timestamp behind every rating. It's a starting point you review, adjust with your professional judgment, and sign.

04

Record-keeping, handled

The signed summary and assessment are your record of the meeting — stored securely for a minimum of seven years to meet your record-keeping obligations.

05

100% Australian owned

Recording, transcription and AI all run onshore in Sydney (AWS). Your client's conversation never leaves the country — end to end.
