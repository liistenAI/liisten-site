Why Liisten

Purpose-built for Australian compliance.

Plenty of tools transcribe a meeting. Liisten goes further for Australian firms — mapping the conversation to the AUSTRAC framework, citing the evidence behind every rating, and keeping the whole thing onshore.

FEATURE · 01

Consent-first recording

Every session opens with a consent script. Nothing is recorded until the client agrees — captured and time-stamped in the record.

FEATURE · 02

AUSTRAC-mapped assessment

Seven risk factors scored against the AUSTRAC framework — client type, structure, cash, geography, source of funds, PEP indicators and more — each with a verbatim quote.

FEATURE · 03

Onshore in Sydney

Recording, transcription and AI all run in Australia (AWS Sydney). Your client's conversation never leaves the country.

How it works

Three steps. You stay in control.

Liisten does the groundwork so you don't take notes during the meeting — but the assessment is a starting point you review and sign, never a decision made for you.

1

Record, with consent

Set the phone down, read the consent line, and have the conversation. Liisten captures it — nothing staged, nothing to type.

2

AI does the groundwork

It transcribes, identifies who said what, and drafts a preliminary risk assessment mapped to AUSTRAC — with the evidence behind every rating.

3

You review & sign

Adjust any rating with your professional judgment, close out follow-ups, then sign and send. Your signed review is the record of file.
