Good to know

Pricing questions, answered.

When am I charged?

Only when you approve and send a report. Recording and reviewing are always free — you'll never lose a meeting because a balance ran out, and re-sending never costs extra.

Do credits expire?

No. Buy a pack once and use it whenever. There are no monthly fees and no lock-in contracts.

Do I pay per person?

No. Credits are shared across your whole firm, and you can add as many practitioners as you like at no extra cost.

General meeting vs onboarding meeting?

A general meeting costs 1 credit and produces the file note. An onboarding meeting — the file note plus the AUSTRAC-mapped risk assessment — costs 2.

Is there really a free trial?

Yes — your firm's first 10 meetings are free. They're recorded, reviewed and fully visible in the app, so you can see exactly what you get before you buy a credit.

How do I pay?

Securely by card through our billing portal, a normal business purchase. Prices are in AUD and exclude GST. Questions? Email us.
