Privacy Policy

How Liisten handles personal information — consistent with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. The short version: consent first, processed onshore, never used to train AI models, and yours to delete.

Effective 27 July 2026 · Liisten (“we”, “us”) — Australian owned, hosted in Sydney.

1. Scope

This policy covers the Liisten app, the dashboard at app.liisten.com.au, and this website. It explains what personal information we collect, why, where it lives, and your choices — consistent with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

2. What we collect

  • Account details — your name, work email, firm name and business type.
  • Meeting recordings — the audio you record, always after spoken consent is captured at the start of the recording.
  • Generated records — the transcript, file note and preliminary risk assessment produced from a recording.
  • Client details — the information your firm enters about its clients to build its compliance records.

3. How we use it

Solely to provide the service: to transcribe and analyse your meetings, make the results available to your firm in the dashboard, notify you when a report is ready, and support your account. We do not use your recordings, transcripts or reports to train AI models, and we do not sell personal information.

4. Storage and location

Recordings, transcripts and reports are processed and stored on Australian onshore infrastructure (Sydney), encrypted in transit and at rest. Notification emails — such as “a report is ready to review” — deliberately contain no client details or meeting content, so nothing sensitive leaves that onshore boundary in email.

5. Who we share it with

Only the processors needed to run the service, under contract and not permitted to use your data for their own purposes:

  • a transcription and speaker-diarisation provider and an AI analysis provider, operating onshore, for meeting content;
  • a transactional email provider, for account emails that carry no meeting or client content;
  • a payments provider, when you buy credits — your card details go directly to them and never touch our servers.

6. Retention

Meeting audio is retained only as long as needed to process and support the record. Signed reports and file notes are firm records: they're retained securely for at least seven years, consistent with AML/CTF record-keeping obligations, and remain under your firm's control. You can delete meetings and their audio from within the app, subject to records your firm is required to keep.

7. Your rights

You can access and correct your personal information, and request deletion, subject to any records your firm must keep under its professional and AML/CTF obligations. If you believe we've mishandled your information, contact us and we'll investigate; you can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

8. Contact

Privacy enquiries: hello@liisten.com.au. We'll respond within a reasonable period, usually a few business days.
