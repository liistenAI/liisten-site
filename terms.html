Terms & Conditions

The terms that govern your firm's use of Liisten. The short version: you stay the accountable professional, Liisten drafts and documents, you review and approve — and we've tried to say all of it in plain English.

Effective 28 July 2026 · Liisten (“we”, “us”) — Australian owned, hosted in Sydney.

1. About these terms

These terms are an agreement between Liisten and the firm that creates an account. By creating a firm account, or accepting an invitation to join one, you agree to them. If you create the account on a firm's behalf, you confirm you're authorised to bind that firm. If you don't agree, don't use the service.

2. What Liisten is

Liisten records client meetings with spoken consent, transcribes them, and produces a file note and — for onboarding meetings — a preliminary risk assessment mapped to AUSTRAC indicators. Recordings are made in the Liisten app; your firm reviews, adjusts and approves the results in the dashboard at app.liisten.com.au. Liisten is a drafting and documentation tool. It is not legal, compliance, tax or financial advice, and it does not perform identity verification, PEP or sanctions screening.

3. Your responsibilities

  • You remain accountable. Your firm is the reporting entity under its AML/CTF and professional obligations. Every Liisten output is preliminary until a practitioner in your firm has reviewed and approved it, and responsibility for the final record rests with your firm.
  • Consent before recording. You're responsible for obtaining all-party consent before recording a meeting, in line with the surveillance-device laws that apply where the meeting takes place. The in-app consent step helps you capture that consent on the record; it doesn't discharge the obligation for you.
  • Screening stays with your provider. Identity verification, PEP and sanctions screening are carried out by your firm through its own provider — Liisten's output is designed to sit alongside them, not replace them.
  • Account security. Keep sign-in credentials confidential, and remove team members promptly when they leave your firm.

4. Accounts and roles

Firm accounts are created on the web with a business email address. A firm has one Owner, who manages billing and can transfer ownership; compliance officers and team members have the permissions shown in the dashboard. Seats are unlimited and free — you pay for reports, not people.

5. Credits and billing

  • Your firm's first ten meetings are processed free, with full visibility of the output.
  • After that, reports draw on prepaid credits, purchased in the dashboard at the prices shown on our pricing page. Prices are in AUD and exclude GST unless stated otherwise.
  • A recording is only charged when it processes successfully into a report. If processing fails, nothing is charged, and recording itself is never blocked.
  • Payments are handled by our payments provider; your card details go directly to them and never touch our servers. Optional auto top-up, if the Owner enables it, reuses a card your firm has already paid with and can be switched off at any time.

6. Your content and ours

Your firm owns its recordings, transcripts, file notes, assessments and client records. You grant us the licence needed to host and process them solely to provide the service — we don't use them to train AI models, and how we handle personal information is set out in our Privacy Policy. We own the Liisten software, brand and site; nothing in these terms transfers that to you.

7. Acceptable use

Don't use Liisten to record anyone without the consent the law requires, don't attempt to breach or probe the security of the service, and don't resell or provide the service to third parties outside your firm. We may suspend accounts that put other customers or the service at risk.

8. Accuracy and availability

Transcription and analysis are automated and can contain errors — that's precisely why every report requires practitioner review and approval before it becomes a firm record. The service is provided “as is” and we don't promise uninterrupted availability, though we build and host it to a standard we'd want for our own compliance records.

9. Liability

To the extent permitted by law, Liisten is not liable for decisions made in reliance on its output, or for indirect or consequential loss; where liability can't be excluded, it's limited to resupplying the service or the amount your firm paid us in the previous twelve months, at our election. Nothing in these terms excludes rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law.

10. Ending the agreement

You can stop using Liisten and close your firm account at any time. On closure we'll make your firm's records available for export for a reasonable period, then delete them, subject to the retention described in the Privacy Policy and any records your firm is required to keep. We may close accounts that materially breach these terms.

11. Changes

We may update these terms as the service evolves. If a change is material we'll notify your firm's Owner by email before it takes effect; continuing to use the service after that is acceptance of the updated terms.

12. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws in force in Australia, and disputes are subject to the jurisdiction of the Australian courts.
